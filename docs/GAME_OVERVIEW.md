# Ashen Pity Game Overview

## Current Build

`Ashen Pity - Combat Identity v0.3.0` is a Stage 1.3 combat sandbox. It is not a full roguelite loop yet.

The player enters the existing shrine route and fights a repeatable Ash Trial in the central arena. The goal of this milestone is combat identity and presentation: quick movement, readable enemy danger, stronger feedback, and the first signature mechanic.

## Core Loop

1. Start from the title screen.
2. Enter the shrine route.
3. Fight the Ash Trial waves.
4. Use movement, dodge, combo attacks, heavy attacks, and Ash Brand Collect opportunities.
5. Defeat the Bell-Bearer elite.
6. See the Ash Trial Complete summary.
7. Replay immediately or return to title.

## Signature Mechanic: Ash Brand

Ash Brand is earned through timing.

- A perfect dodge through an enemy attack Brands that enemy.
- A Branded enemy gets a visible ash/ember ring and particles.
- Light attacks against the Branded enemy build Collect readiness.
- When Collect is ready, the HUD shows the `Q COLLECT` prompt.
- Pressing `Q` performs a dash-through cross-cut attack, consumes the Brand, deals high damage, and applies high stagger.

Collect cannot be used freely. It requires a valid Branded enemy, enough follow-up hits, range, and stamina.

## Player Moves

- Light combo: three fast mouse-aimed strikes with different reach, width, damage, and lunge.
- Heavy attack: slower, higher stamina cost, stronger damage and stagger.
- Dodge: fast movement with a tuned invulnerability window and perfect-dodge check.
- Flask: two charges, committed heal animation, interruptible by damage.
- Collect: high-impact dash-through payoff against a primed Branded enemy.

## Ash Trial Encounter

The trial uses the existing shrine arena and keeps the project small.

- Wave 1: two Shrine Guardians.
- Wave 2: one Ashbound Hound and one Shrine Guardian.
- Wave 3: one Reliquary Archer and one Shrine Guardian.
- Final wave: one Bell-Bearer elite.

The Bell-Bearer is an elite test enemy, not a full boss.

## Out Of Scope

Stage 1.3 intentionally does not include relics, pity systems, currency, shops, permanent progression, weapon selection, procedural generation, save data, multiple playable characters, or a full boss.
